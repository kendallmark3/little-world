# Project Intent
Purpose: The Little World — a living, browser-based civilization simulation that is worth watching, that the player influences rather than controls, and that grows over time into a laboratory for agriculture, economics, game theory, rates of change, and physics.
Business outcomes: Curiosity, discovery, and enjoyment. A natural lab where those disciplines arise from what actually happens in the settlement, not from invented use cases. Possibly an iOS app someday.
Constraints: Browser-first, no required backend, no paid APIs, no LLM or external AI in the simulation, deterministic seeded rules, minimum sufficient complexity. Evolve by Progressive Intent: one intent at a time, owner decides continue or stop.
